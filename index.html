How to Play

What is Dual N-Back?

Dual N-Back is a cognitive training exercise designed to improve your working memory and fluid intelligence. You'll track two sequences simultaneously: visual positions and audio letters.

How It Works

  1. A square will appear in different positions on the grid
  2. At the same time, you'll hear letters spoken (C, H, K, L, Q, R, S, T)
  3. Your job is to remember if the current stimulus matches what you saw/heard N steps back
  4. For example, in 2-Back:
    • Press L if the current position matches the position from 2 steps ago
    • Press A if the current letter matches the letter from 2 steps ago
    • Press both if both match!

Controls

A Audio match (current letter matches N steps back)
L Position match (current position matches N steps back)

On mobile devices, use the on-screen buttons.

Level Progression

Your N-level will automatically adjust based on your performance:

  • Score ≥ 90%: Level increases (harder)
  • Score < 50%: Level decreases (easier)
  • Score 50-89%: Level stays the same

Tips

  • Start with 2-Back and let the game adjust to your level
  • Stay focused - this exercise requires sustained attention
  • Train regularly for best results (daily 20-minute sessions recommended)
  • Don't get discouraged - it's challenging by design!
